The Long March

Three layers of the network to the Motherboard, one life. Both sides flip, and the higher card hits for the difference in rank. Ties start a war: three down, one up, and the winner captures the loser's best card. Choose your route: recruits, drafts and tests for new cards, then chargers, labs, black boxes and strange hackers along the way, then a fight. Build your deck as you go.
